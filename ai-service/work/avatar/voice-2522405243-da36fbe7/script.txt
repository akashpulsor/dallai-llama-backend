If procrastination were really about willpower, why do we avoid the tasks that matter most? Psychologically, procrastination is often emotional avoidance. A task can trigger fear of failure, perfectionism, uncertainty, or overwhelm. Avoiding it gives your brain quick relief, so the cycle gets reinforced. Instead of asking, "Why am I so lazy?" ask, "What feeling am I avoiding?" Then shrink the next step: open the document, write one line, or work for just two minutes. Progress begins with emotional safety, not self-punishment.
